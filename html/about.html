About Our College

Maratha Vidya Prasarak Samaj's Karmaveer Adv. Baburao Ganpatrao Thakare College of Engineering (KBTCOE) is an autonomous engineering institute located in the heart of Nashik, Maharashtra


. Established in 1999, the college is spread across an 11-acre campus and is permanently affiliated with Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU). The institute holds an 'A++' grade accreditation from NAAC and features several NBA-accredited programs.

Our Vision

To be an internationally accredited, multidisciplinary, and multi-collaborative institute working on a technology-enabled platform, fostering innovations and patents through a state-of-the-art academic system designed by highly qualified faculty for the development of the common masses at large..

Our Mission

  • Provide quality education.
  • Encourage innovation and research.
  • Develop leadership and teamwork skills.
  • Support students in career development.
